# Engineering Calculator

Engineering Calculator is a compact field instrument for checking a beam-load scenario and keeping the assumptions visible beside the result. The first surface should make inputs, units, formula, result, and audit notes feel like one trustworthy instrument.

## Audience and scene

An engineer or technically minded builder doing a quick illustrative check at a desk or on site. Values are synthetic and educational; the interface must never imply a stamped design or safety certification.

## Success

The user can choose a load case, adjust span and load, run the calculation, and read the result with units and assumptions without hunting through a dashboard.

## Boundaries

- Keep the product self-contained and client-side.
- Use a clearly labeled illustrative simply-supported beam formula.
- Make load-case selection, input changes, calculate, and reset work.
- Keep disclaimer and assumptions visible; do not claim engineering approval.
